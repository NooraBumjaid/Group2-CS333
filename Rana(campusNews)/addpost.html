New Computer Science Program Launch

By Dr. Sarah Johnson | 2024-03-15

Engineering

The university is proud to announce the launch of a new Computer Science program focusing on Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning. This cutting-edge program will prepare students for the future of technology.

The program will include courses taught by leading experts in the field, hands-on projects with industry partners, and opportunities for research in our newly established AI Lab.

Students will learn about neural networks, deep learning, natural language processing, computer vision, and other cutting-edge topics in AI and ML.

Applications for the program will open next month, and classes will begin in the Fall semester.

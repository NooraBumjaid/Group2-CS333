The News Title

By The Author | Date: 10/3/2025

Department: Technology

The College of Engineering has just announced the launch of a new "Introduction to Information Technology" course, designed to equip students with foundational skills in modern technology. This hands-on class covers essential topics such as computer systems, networking basics, cybersecurity principles, and an introduction to programming languages like Python and JavaScript. Students will also explore emerging technologies like artificial intelligence, cloud computing, and data analytics, preparing them for diverse career paths in the ever-evolving IT industry. With a focus on practical applications and real-world problem-solving, this course is ideal for beginners looking to build a strong technological foundation while fostering creativity and innovation. Whether you're pursuing a career in software development, IT support, or tech entrepreneurship, this class promises to be a valuable stepping stone.
